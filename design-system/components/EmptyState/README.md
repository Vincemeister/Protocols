# EmptyState

What a screen shows when there is nothing yet: a 32px Lucide icon in `ink-faint`, a `heading-5` that says what will appear here ("Your lists show up here"), one `body-sm` sentence in `ink-muted`, and at most one button. Centred in its column with `space-12` vertical padding. Used on Saved, Notifications, the Products and Lists tabs of a new profile, and Search with no results (no button there — the field is the action).

The consumer provides `icon`, `title`, `message`, `action` (Button props). Don't: illustrations or mascots; more than one sentence; a sad tone ("Nothing here yet…").
